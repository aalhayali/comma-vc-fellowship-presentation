Energy Infrastructure
VC thesis

The next AI bottleneck is not chips. It is usable megawatts.

A simple VC thesis: AI needs power, the grid is slow, and startups can help close the gap.

Abdullah Al-Hayali
5-minute discussion deck · May 2026
Open with the simple point: AI feels digital, but it runs on power. The next bottleneck may be electricity, not chips.
01 / why now
Macro facts

Electricity demand is growing again.

5×U.S. power demand forecasts jumped from 23 GW to 128 GW in two years.
4.4%Data centers used 4.4% of U.S. electricity in 2023. DOE expects 6.7–12% by 2028.
2.6TWNearly 2,600 GW of new power projects are waiting to connect to the grid.
19%Only ~19% of older queued projects actually got built.
Sources: Grid Strategies, Strategic Industries Surging, Dec 2024; DOE/LBNL Data Center Energy Use Report, Dec 2024; LBNL Queued Up 2024.
Use this slide to show the problem: demand is rising, but connecting new power is painfully slow.
02 / macro thesis
The constraint has shifted

AI needs a lot more electricity. The grid was not built for that speed.

Power availability is becoming the constraint for new AI data centers.
The grid is slow. Wires, substations, transformers, and studies take years.
Reliable clean power is worth more when customers need power every hour.
Flexibility matters. Batteries, EVs, HVAC, and factories can shift demand when the grid is tight.
Macro conclusion
More power matters. Power you can actually use matters more.
Say it plainly: lots of power projects exist on paper. The money is in getting real power to real sites.
03 / where value accrues
Bottleneck stack

Follow the bottlenecks.

Load

AI + industry

Data centers, factories, EVs, and heat pumps are adding demand in specific places.

Power

Firm capacity

Data centers need power that is available when they need it.

Wires

Grid access

Projects get stuck waiting for grid connection, wires, transformers, and studies.

Operations

Flexibility

Batteries, EV chargers, HVAC, and factories can reduce or shift load.

Software

Decision layer

Software can speed up siting, forecasts, inspections, procurement, and dispatch.

Investable question
Does the startup help customers get power faster or use the grid better?
This keeps the thesis focused. The question is not “what is cool in climate?” It is “where is the grid stuck?”
04 / micro thesis
Where I would look

Micro thesis: back tools that make the grid less stuck.

1. Interconnection intelligence

Help developers find better sites and avoid bad grid queues.

2. Flexible interconnection

Let projects connect sooner if they agree to use less power during grid stress.

3. Grid-enhancing tech

Use sensors and software to move more power through existing lines.

4. VPP / DER orchestration

Group batteries, EV chargers, thermostats, and buildings into a flexible power resource.

5. Transformer + substation stack

Find, buy, monitor, and reuse scarce transformers and substation equipment.

6. Data-center power OS

Help data centers find power, buy power, and manage backup + storage.

Pattern
Prefer software. Add hardware only when it gives useful data or control.
This is the core VC answer. Not “build more solar.” More like: help projects get through the grid faster.
05 / AI in the space
Demand shock + tooling
AI is creating the power problem. It can also help fix parts of it.

The bad version is a generic utility chatbot. The good version helps operate real assets and saves real money.

New demand: data centers used ~460 TWh globally in 2022 and could pass 1,000 TWh by 2026.
Faster work: AI can help with studies, permits, forecasts, inspections, and outage response.
Better control: AI is useful when it controls batteries, HVAC, EV charging, backup power, or factory load.
Moat: private grid data, deep integrations, audit trails, and real control of assets.
Sources: IEA Electricity 2024; Goldman Sachs AI power demand, 2024; DOE/LBNL data center report.
This is the sober AI point: AI creates demand, but AI startups only matter here if they touch real utility workflows or assets.
06 / market signals
What hyperscalers are already doing

Big tech is buying power directly.

Microsoft × Constellation

20-year deal to restart Three Mile Island Unit 1. About 835 MW, targeted for 2028.

Amazon / AWS nuclear

Bought a data-center campus next to a nuclear plant; also announced new nuclear projects.

Google × Kairos

Agreement to buy power from small nuclear reactors, starting around 2030.

Google × Fervo

Geothermal is attractive because it can run all day, unlike wind or solar.

Meta × Sage

Deal for up to 150 MW of geothermal power later this decade.

OpenAI Stargate

Up to $500B announced for U.S. AI infrastructure. Data centers are now mega-projects.

Read-through
If big tech is fighting for power, startups that help get power faster matter.
The point is not “VC should fund nuclear plants.” The point is that power access is now strategic.

08 / highest conviction wedges
If I had to pick 3

The three areas I would spend time on.

Interconnection + siting intelligence

Why: bad grid queues can kill projects.

  • Start: developer workflow + project data
  • Later: financing, permits, power contracts, equipment

Data-center power orchestration

Why: AI data centers need a lot of power now.

  • Start: find power + manage flexible load
  • Later: storage, backup, grid services

Grid capacity optimization

Why: new transmission is slow, and existing wires are underused.

  • Start: sensors + dynamic line ratings
  • Later: utility planning + congestion tools
Micro conclusion
Back startups that help customers get power faster.
Simple punchline: get power faster. Examples if asked: Paces/Pearl Street/GridUnity, Gridmatic/FlexGen/Leap, LineVision/Heimdall/NewGrid.
09 / what could break the thesis
Risks

Utility sales cycles

Utility sales can take 12–24 months. Start with faster buyers when possible.

Regulatory friction

Regulation can slow everything down. The product needs clear audit trails.

Hardware liability

Hardware touches safety, warranty, and cyber risk. Reliability has to be real.

Demand overbuild risk

Some data-center forecasts will be wrong. The startup should still work if AI demand cools.

Risk posture
Do not make the thesis depend on AI hype.
This slide adds skepticism. It makes the deck more credible.
10 / closing thesis
One-line takeaway
Final answer

Energy infrastructure is now compute infrastructure.

The opportunity is simple: help new demand get power faster, and help the grid use what it already has.

Macro

Power demand is rising again. Grid buildout is slow.

Micro

Back startups that speed up grid connection and flexibility.

AI

AI adds demand. Useful AI helps operate the grid.

Thank you
